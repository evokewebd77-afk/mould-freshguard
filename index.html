A Treatment Plan Built Around the Affected Space

The size of the affected area, materials and conditions inside the property determine the treatment required. Our certified training helps us understand the health risks of mold, assess what needs attention and recommend the safest, most effective solution.

01

Assessment before treatment

Share the location of the mold, how long it has been present and whether it has returned. Photos can help with the initial discussion; an on-site assessment may still be needed.

02

Professional mold treatment

Discuss suitable remediation for the affected areas. Where appropriate, the proposed approach may include fogging as part of a broader treatment plan. The agreed scope should make clear what will be treated.

03

Moisture and prevention advice

Ask about moisture, condensation and ventilation factors that may need attention. Understanding these issues helps you plan follow-up work alongside mold treatment.

Frequently asked questions

Questions About Professional Mold Removal

How much does mold removal cost?

The quotation depends on the affected area, access, materials and proposed treatment. Send a description and photos to start the discussion. If an inspection is required before a quote, ask about its cost when arranging it.

Can you give advice from photos?

Photos can support an initial discussion, but they may not show hidden moisture or the full extent of the problem. We can discuss whether an on-site assessment is appropriate.

Can mold return after treatment?

Recurrence is possible if conditions that support mold remain. The treatment discussion should include relevant moisture and ventilation issues and any separate work that may be needed.

Do I need to leave the property during treatment?

This depends on the method and areas being treated. Ask for the preparation, access, ventilation and re-entry instructions before work starts, including arrangements for children and pets.

Do you treat rental and commercial properties?

Enquiries are welcome from homeowners, landlords and businesses. Include the property type, location and access arrangements so the requirements can be discussed.

Is an assessment free?

Assessment arrangements depend on location and the complexity of the enquiry. Confirm the format and any charges before booking; do not assume every on-site visit is free.
